Common questions

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings → Delete account. Your account, goals, sessions, island and friend links are erased permanently. There is no recovery, so only do this if you mean it.

How do I delete a single session?

Open Stats → pick a week → tap the bin next to the session. Useful when a check-in was logged because you drove past a place.

Auto Check-In is not counting my visits

  • iOS needs Always location access for this. Check iOS Settings → Goals → Location.
  • A visit only counts once you stay longer than the minimum you set — passing by is never a visit.
  • If your pin sits in the middle of a large building, move it to the entrance and try a smaller radius.

I do not want to share my location at all

Auto Check-In is optional. Skip it during setup, or turn it off later under Settings → Turn off Auto Check-In. The Focus Timer works fully without location.

How do friends work?

Open Friends and share your six-character code, or enter someone else's. Friends see your week, your total hours and your island — nothing else. You can remove a friend at any time.

What is the difference between Intervals and Flowtime?

Intervals runs a fixed block and then starts a short break by itself. Flowtime never ends on its own: you turn the ring to set a target, and the clock keeps counting until you stop. Switch between them in Settings → Focus style.

My island stopped growing

Each island size has room for a limited number of objects per category. When a category is full, pick another one before starting a session — or keep going until the island grows to the next size.
